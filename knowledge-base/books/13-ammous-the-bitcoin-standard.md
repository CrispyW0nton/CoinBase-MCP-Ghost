# Ammous — *The Bitcoin Standard*

**Why it matters here:** the thesis for *why hold BTC at all* and the
long-horizon, low-time-preference posture that should temper any short-term
trading the MCP enables. Also grounds the "observe, don't custody recklessly"
stance.

---

## Ch.1 — Money
- Money = most salable good across time/space/scale. **Hardness = stock-to-flow
  ratio**; high S2F preserves value.
- **MCP usage:** frames BTC as a store-of-value first; trading around a core
  long-term hold should not jeopardize the hold.

## Ch.2 — Primitive Moneys
- Every easily-produced money (Rai stones, shells, beads) collapsed once supply
  could expand. Hardness is the survival trait.

## Ch.3 — Monetary Metals
- Gold won via ~1.5% annual supply growth and indestructibility → enabled
  long-term thinking.

## Ch.4 — Government Money
- Fiat (post-WWI) is easy money; hyperinflation is unique to it.
- **MCP usage:** the macro case for a non-discretionary asset; relevant to
  position-holding conviction during drawdowns.

## Ch.5 — Money and Time Preference
- Hard money → low time preference → saving, capital formation. Easy money →
  high time preference → debt/consumption.
- **MCP rule (behavioral):** prefer patient, low-churn strategies; high-turnover
  churn is the "high time preference" trap and pays the spread repeatedly
  (ties to Harris fees, Kahneman impulsivity).

## Ch.6 — Capitalism's Information System
- Prices are knowledge; central-bank rate manipulation breaks the signal,
  causing boom/bust.
- **MCP usage:** trust *market* prices from the live book over narratives;
  the order book is the price-discovery signal.

## Ch.7 — Sound Money & Freedom
- Sound money is freely chosen, beyond political control.

## Ch.8 — Digital Money
- Bitcoin's **difficulty adjustment** caps S2F growth: rising value cannot raise
  supply. "The hardest money ever invented."

## Ch.9 — What Is Bitcoin Good For?
- Absolute scarcity (21M), portability, censorship/seizure resistance → premier
  long-term store of value — **provided you secure your own keys.**

## Ch.10 — Bitcoin Questions
- Energy/scaling/immutability debates don't undermine the core thesis. Expect
  high short-term volatility; the long-term thesis rewards patience.
- **MCP rules:**
  - "Your first investment is time spent learning to buy/store/own securely"
    (p.7). → The MCP never custodies and never touches keys; custody is the
    user's responsibility on Coinbase, with its own risks (see Ch.10 / custody
    notes).
  - Size for ~80% drawdown survivability (echoes `Mastering Crypto Assets`).
