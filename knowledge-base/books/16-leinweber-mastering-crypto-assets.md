# Leinweber, Willig & Schoenfeld — *Mastering Crypto Assets* (supplemental)

**Why it matters here:** the practical valuation, classification, allocation, and
risk-sizing rulebook for the assets the MCP observes on Coinbase.

---

## Ch.1–2 — Introduction / Bitcoin history
- Context for crypto as an emerging asset class.

## Ch.3 — Taxonomy of Crypto Assets
- Eight categories: DeFi, Exchange tokens, Infrastructure apps, Media/Metaverse,
  Payments, Smart-contract platforms, Stablecoins, Store of Value.
- **MCP rule:** tag every observed pair by category; valuation method and risk
  follow from the category.

## Ch.4 — Valuation
- **Metcalfe's Law** (value ∝ N² users), **Stock-to-Flow** (scarcity),
  **DCF** (for fee/staking-revenue tokens), **TVL** (DeFi).
- **MCP rule:** never price a store-of-value token with a cash-flow model or
  vice-versa; match the framework to the taxonomy.

## Ch.5 — Crypto as an Asset Class
- A 1–5% allocation historically improved portfolio Sharpe without exploding
  drawdown.

## Ch.6 — Asset Allocation *(critical sizing rules)*
- **Rule of 1%:** start with a bearable-loss allocation (1–2%).
- **Risk-per-trade:** ≤ 0.5–1% of total capital on any single bet.
- **Always be undersized** to survive 80%+ drawdowns.
- **Rebalance quarterly** (forces buy-low/sell-high).
- Dynamic risk management: 200-day MA trend + momentum filters to avoid deep
  bear markets.
- **MCP rule:** these caps sit *on top of* Kelly (Chan Ch.8) and `maxNotionalUsd`
  — whichever is smallest wins.

## Ch.7 — Staking to Earning
- Stake PoS tokens (e.g. ETH) to offset inflation dilution; vet validator
  uptime/slashing history. (Informational; the MCP does not stake.)

## Ch.8 — Index Investments
- Prefer market-cap-weighted indexes over single-altcoin alpha (most active
  managers underperform after costs).

## Ch.9 — Outlook
- Use **clean pricing**: volume-weighted median across vetted venues to avoid
  fat-finger/manipulation. → For the MCP, trust the Coinbase book but be aware a
  single-venue price can be an outlier.

### Due-diligence gate (before trading any non-major)
Tokenomics (supply/inflation) · Technology standards · Codebase audits/bug
bounties · Regulatory standing · Proof-of-reserves for the venue.
