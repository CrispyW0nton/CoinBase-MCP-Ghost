# Burniske & Tatar — *Cryptoassets*

**Why it matters here:** the investor's framework for classifying and valuing the
assets on Coinbase, and for fitting them into a portfolio.

## Part 1 — What (taxonomy)
- **Cryptocurrencies vs crypto-commodities vs crypto-tokens.** Classify each
  observed pair before applying any valuation.

## Part 2 — Why (valuation & portfolio)
- Network value frameworks (Metcalfe, NVT — network-value-to-transactions),
  utility value, and store-of-value theses.
- Crypto's **low correlation** historically improved portfolio efficiency at
  small allocations (mirrors *Mastering Crypto Assets* Ch.5).
- **MCP rule:** size crypto as a small, capped sleeve; rebalance with discipline.

## Part 3 — How (practical)
- Due diligence, exchange/wallet selection, custody, and the importance of
  understanding what you own before buying.
- Watch on-chain health metrics; don't trade narratives alone (Kahneman).
