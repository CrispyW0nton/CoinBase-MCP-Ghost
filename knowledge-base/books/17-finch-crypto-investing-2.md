**Why it matters here:** beginner-safety rails and scam-avoidance the agent
should enforce when a human acts on MCP output.

## Ch.1–2 — Foundation / Landscape
- Understand asset basics and the market structure before acting.

## Ch.3–4 — Security & Risk Management *(critical)*
- Hardware wallets (cold storage) for anything held > a few weeks.
- Mandatory 2FA (non-SMS), strong passphrases, firmware updates.
- **Enable withdrawal whitelisting** on exchanges.
- Never share recovery phrases; avoid public Wi-Fi for transactions.

## Ch.5 — Market Strategies
- **Dollar-cost averaging (DCA):** fixed amount at regular intervals to smooth
  volatility. → A patient, low-time-preference default (echoes Ammous Ch.5).
- Stop-losses to protect capital; diversify across majors + stablecoins.

## Ch.6 — Technology
- Whitepaper/roadmap scrutiny before any altcoin.

## Ch.7 — Legal & Tax
- Track cost basis and obligations (informational; the MCP does not).

## Ch.8 — Life-Stage Strategies
- Match risk to horizon/age.

## Ch.9–13 — Myths / Advanced / Case Studies / Community / Future-proofing
- Recognize and avoid scams.

### Red flags the agent must enforce
- Guaranteed high returns / "no risk."
- Anonymous teams; opaque whitepapers.
- Pressure to invest *now*.
- Pump-and-dump on low-cap coins.
- Phishing links for credentials.

### Concrete agent rules
1. Hardware wallet for holds > a few weeks.
2. Never invest more than the user can afford to lose.
3. Mandate non-SMS 2FA everywhere.
4. Whitepaper audit before any altcoin.
5. Withdrawal whitelisting on.
6. Rule of scarcity: prefer finite-supply majors (BTC).
